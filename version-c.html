00 · Before you share

Understand what you are agreeing to.

Review the information below before granting Aiko access. Each data category is optional. You can choose only what is needed and change your decision later.

Important: This is a prototype. It does not connect to your accounts, collect real personal data, store information on a server or share data with third parties. The retention and third-party terms below are proposed policies for a future product, not implemented guarantees.

2. How long would data be kept?

Proposed retention schedule for the future product. The final periods should be confirmed by the product owner and disclosed before launch.

Location used for a travel estimateDiscard after the estimate; no location history by default.
Calendar, task and message contentProcess only what is needed; retain derived scheduling context for up to 30 days, then delete or ask you to renew.
Access and security logsKeep for up to 90 days to help you review access and investigate misuse.
Revoking permission should stop future access. It does not automatically erase information already stored. A separate delete-data control should let you request deletion.

3. Who can access your data?

Proposed access rules for a production service.

Aiko (the AI assistant)May process only the categories and purposes you approve, within the scope you select.
Service providersOnly providers needed to operate the service; access should be limited, protected by contractual safeguards and disclosed by name or category.
Advertisers and data brokersNo access; personal data should not be sold or shared for targeted advertising.
Other people or appsNo access by default. Any new sharing destination should require a separate explanation and choice.
Before launch, the product should publish the actual provider list, processing locations, legal basis and any applicable international data transfers. These details are not yet specified in this prototype.
